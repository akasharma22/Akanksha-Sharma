## 📊 Featured Projects

### 🍺 Alcohol Consumption & Life Expectancy in South & Southeast Asia
**Course:** Econometrics | University of Massachusetts Amherst

- Analyzed WHO panel data across 20 countries (2000–2015) using RStudio
- Built segmented regression models separating low (<1.0L) and high (>1.0L) alcohol consumption countries
- Used log-transformed GDP and interaction terms to assess regional differences between South & Southeast Asia
- Key finding: GDP is the strongest predictor of life expectancy; alcohol consumption showed weak, insignificant effects (R² ≈ 0.000067 for low consumers)
- Visualized results using **ggplot2** and R Markdown

`R Studio` `ggplot2` `OLS Regression` `WHO Data` `R Markdown`

### 💼 Performance-Based Pay & Worker Output — Prolific Field Experiment
**Course:** ResEcon 428 | University of Massachusetts Amherst  
**Team:** Abha Dewal, Anjalika Arora, Akanksha Sharma

- Designed and ran a natural field experiment on Prolific.com with 44 participants across two incentive conditions
- Compared fixed pay ($2.00) vs. bonus-eligible pay ($1.50 + $0.50 bonus) on writing quantity and quality
- Key findings:
  - Bonus group wrote **~103 more words** on average (p < 0.001, R² = 68.5%)
  - Quality scores increased by **3.01 points** (p = 0.0002)
  - Error rate reduced by **0.034 per 10 words** (p = 0.0076)
- Controlled for demographics (gender, ethnicity, English proficiency) using OLS regression

`OLS Regression` `T-tests` `Qualtrics` `Prolific` `Excel` `Experimental Design`
